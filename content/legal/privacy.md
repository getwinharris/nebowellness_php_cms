# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains how the platform collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our website, products, support channels, or account.

## 1. Information We Collect

- Account details such as name, email, phone number, password hash, and saved addresses.
- Order, shipping, payment-status, and support records. Full card, bank, and UPI credentials are handled by payment providers and are not stored by us.
- Contact form and support messages, including the information you choose to include.
- Device, browser, IP, usage, cookie, and referral information needed for security, analytics, and site operation.

## 2. Google Sign-In and Analytics

When you use Google Sign-In, we receive the basic profile information permitted by the consent screen: name, email address, and profile image. We do not request access to Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or other Google services. Analytics and advertising tools may receive anonymised usage information such as pages visited, device type, referral source, and general location.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, fulfil orders, process payments, answer support requests, send transactional updates, prevent abuse, improve the service, and meet legal, accounting, and tax obligations. Promotional messages are sent only where permitted and include an opt-out path.

## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Essential cookies support authentication, security, cart state, preferences, and session continuity. Optional audience measurement is off by default. When the clinic enables it, signed-out visitors can choose Analytics and advertising separately under audience measurement preferences on this page. Choices last up to six months in the current browser and can be changed here. Measurement is limited to the homepage and About page. For links labeled Google, Facebook, or Instagram, Analytics can count the referring platform. The site does not send enquiry forms, support messages, account details, therapy pages, search terms, or query strings to measurement providers.

## 5. Sharing and Service Providers

We share the minimum information required with hosting, database, email, analytics, authentication, shipping, support, and payment providers. Providers must handle information under their own applicable terms and security obligations. We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Retention and Security

Runtime account, order, and audit data is stored in the platform's secured remote database. We apply access controls, record-scoped queries, encryption in transit, audit logging, and operational safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Chat keeps up to 12 completed exchanges per assistant in your current site session. When you ask a follow-up, the assistant can use excerpts from the four most recent exchanges. New chat clears that assistant’s history. History becomes unavailable after one hour without a saved exchange or when your sign-in session changes. Tickets sent to the clinic are handled as separate support records.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You may review or update account information, manage saved addresses, request support, withdraw optional marketing consent, or ask for access, correction, deletion, or restriction where applicable. Some records must be retained for fraud prevention, disputes, tax, or legal compliance.

## 8. Children

The service is intended for adults. Minors may use it only with a parent or legal guardian's supervision and consent.

## 9. Third-Party Links

External websites, payment pages, social networks, and service providers have their own policies. This policy applies to information collected by this platform.

## 10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the product, law, or providers change. The current version and update date are published here. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact and support channels shown on the website.
