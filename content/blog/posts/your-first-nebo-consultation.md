## Start with your goals

Tell us what you would like help with: digestion, metabolic health, lifestyle habits, or maternal and fertility wellness. You can also share what has already been tried and what matters most to you day to day.

## Bring useful context

If you have recent test results, a medication list, or notes about symptoms, keep them nearby for your clinician. You do not need to choose a program before you contact us. The team can explain which services are relevant and what an appointment involves.

## Ask about the plan

Before beginning any program, ask how it fits alongside your existing medical care, what follow-up is offered, and what costs and time commitments to expect. For urgent symptoms, seek prompt medical care instead of waiting for a routine wellness appointment.
