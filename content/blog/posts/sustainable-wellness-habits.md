## Begin with what is realistic

A useful wellness plan should fit your routines and responsibilities. Nebo's lifestyle transformation program considers nutrition, movement, rest, and stress together. The first step is to identify one change you can repeat consistently.

## Review and adjust

Notice how a habit works in your own schedule, then discuss any obstacles with your clinician. Plans can be adjusted as your needs change. If you have a diagnosed condition, are pregnant, or take medication, make changes with your qualified healthcare team.
