## Open the consultation form

Select **Book a consultation** in the desktop header. On your phone, open **Menu** and select **Book a consultation**. Both links take you to the [enquiry form](/contact#contact-form).

## Tell the team how to reach you

Enter your name and email. Choose the program you want to discuss or select **General Question** if you are unsure. Briefly describe what you want to ask the clinic. Leave urgent or sensitive medical information out of the form.

## Send and follow up

If the form shows an email verification field, select **Send code** and enter the code from your inbox. Review your details and select **Send enquiry**. The clinic team will follow up using the contact details you shared. If your need is urgent, contact an appropriate medical service directly rather than waiting for a website response.
