## Start with foods you tolerate

There is no single meal plan that suits every digestive system. Begin with familiar foods that feel comfortable, then build variety gradually. A balanced plate can combine a protein source, cooked vegetables, a carbohydrate that works for you, and a small amount of healthy fat.

## Change one thing at a time

Large changes can make it difficult to understand what helps. Try one practical adjustment, such as eating at regular times or adding one extra portion of vegetables, and notice how your body responds. Keep a brief note of symptoms, sleep, stress, and meals if your practitioner asks for it.

## Get personal guidance when needed

Persistent pain, bleeding, unexplained weight loss, fever, dehydration, or severe symptoms need prompt medical assessment. A qualified clinician can also help if food restriction is increasing or if you have a diagnosed condition.
